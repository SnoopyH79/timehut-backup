Timehut Backup Exporter

Back up your Timehut photos and videos to your own computer

A Chrome / Edge extension that copies the photos and videos in your own Timehut account into a folder you choose — on your PC or an external drive. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Unofficial. Timehut Backup Exporter is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Timehut (時光小屋) in any way. “Timehut” is used only to describe compatibility.

What it does

Full local backup

Saves original photos and videos into a dated folder structure per child.

Incremental

Later runs only download what is new. Files already backed up are skipped.

Resumable

Interrupted downloads continue where they stopped. Nothing is downloaded twice.

Check & repair

Verifies an existing backup and restores missing or deleted files.

How to use: sign in to Timehut in your browser, open the extension, choose a child and a destination folder, then press Start.
